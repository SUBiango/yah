# Publishing Guide — YAH Blog

A step-by-step guide for writing and publishing blog articles. **No coding or
technical knowledge needed** — everything happens in your web browser.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Go to **https://www.yahsl.org/blog/admin/**
2. Click **Login**.
   - First time? You'll receive an email invitation — click the link and set a
     password. If you didn't get one, ask an administrator to invite you.
3. You're now in the content manager.

---

## 2. Create a new article

1. Click **New Blog Post**.
2. Fill in the fields:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The headline of your article. |
| **Slug (optional override)** | Leave **blank** — the web address is created automatically from the title. Only fill this in if you need a specific URL. |
| **Author** | Your name (or the team name). |
| **Publish Date** | The date the article should show. |
| **Featured Image** | The main image (shown on the blog list and at the top of the article). Also used when the article is shared on social media. |
| **Social Share Image (optional)** | Only if you want a *different* image for social media. Otherwise leave blank. |
| **Excerpt** | 1–2 sentence summary. Shown on the blog list and in search results. |
| **Meta Description (optional)** | Leave blank unless you want a different summary for search engines. |
| **Tags** | Optional keywords (e.g. `summit`, `events`). |
| **Draft** | Turn **ON** to keep the article hidden from the public site even after publishing (useful for finishing later). Turn **OFF** to make it public. |
| **Body** | Your article. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, links, lists, and to insert images. |

3. **Adding images inside the article:** in the **Body**, click the image button
   and upload. Images appear inline where you place them.

> **Image rules (important):** photos must be **JPG, PNG, or WEBP**, **under 1MB**,
> and **no wider than 2000 pixels**. Oversized images will block publishing. If a
> photo is too big, resize it before uploading (most phones/computers can
> "reduce size" when exporting). Images are automatically optimized for fast
> loading once published.

---

## 3. Save and move through review

The blog uses an **editorial workflow** with three stages. Use the **status**
dropdown at the top of the editor:

1. **Draft** — your work in progress. Saved, but not on the live site.
2. **In review** — ready for someone to check.
3. **Ready** — approved and ready to go live.

Your work is safe at every stage and **nothing appears on the public site** until
you publish it in the next step. Click **Save** often.

---

## 4. Publish

1. When the article is at the **Ready** stage, click **Publish → Publish now**.
2. The article is saved to the website's content, and the site rebuilds
   automatically.
3. Wait **1–3 minutes**, then check it live at
   **https://www.yahsl.org/blog/**

That's it — your article is published. 🎉

---

## Editing or unpublishing an article

- **To edit:** open the article from the list, make changes, and **Publish** again.
- **To hide an already-published article:** open it, turn the **Draft** toggle
  **ON**, and **Publish**. It will be removed from the public site on the next
  rebuild (but kept in the content manager).

---

## Tips for good articles

- **Titles:** clear and specific — they become the web address and the search-result headline.
- **Excerpt:** treat it as the "teaser" — make someone want to click.
- **Featured image:** use a strong, relevant photo; landscape (wide) images look best.
- **Headings:** break long articles into sections with headings (in the Body toolbar).
- **Proofread** in **Draft** or **In review** before publishing.

---

## Something went wrong?

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Can't log in | Ask an administrator to re-send your invitation. |
| "Publish" seems stuck | Wait a few minutes and refresh; the site rebuilds in the background. |
| Image won't upload / publish fails | The image is likely too large — resize it under 1MB / 2000px wide and try again. |
| Article not showing after 5+ minutes | Check the **Draft** toggle is **OFF**, then Publish again. If still missing, contact an administrator. |
