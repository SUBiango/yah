Welcome to the official blog of Young Access Hub (YAH).

This is where we’ll share stories from the field, updates on our projects, and reflections on what it takes to give young people the tools, skills, and fire to transform their own lives and the communities around them.

We’ve always wanted a dedicated space to write about and share the work we do at YAH, but finding the time to get a blog up and running never quite made it to the top of the list. Until now.

From our work with young people and our annual Youth Empowerment Summit to our upcoming e-learning platform and future projects, we’ll use this space to announce, discuss, and reflect on the work we’re doing.

More than anything, we want this to be a meaningful place where people can learn, find inspiration, and get a closer look at what we’re building and why it matters.

Thank you for reading, and we look forward to sharing more with you, starting with this year’s summit announcement.
